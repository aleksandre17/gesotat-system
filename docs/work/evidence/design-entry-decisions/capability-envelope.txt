6. HYPOTHESIS VERDICTS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  END-TO-END GOVERNED CHAIN ................................ MODIFY
    Not a chain: measured fan-out (225 rows -> 450 files) and fan-in (derived statistic
      from several upstreams). It is a DAG; the linear form is one path through it.
    Not one graph: THREE graphs share the same nodes and differ in direction, mutability
      and lifetime - derivation/provenance (acyclic, append-only), dependency/impact
      (forward, mutable), composition/containment. One edge type constrains none.
    Not a graph database: candidate_2 delivers most traversals with typed FKs; the
      manifest delivers row->file with a typed edge list. Graph-LIKE traversability is
      already achieved relationally at this scale.
    Breaks at cross-boundary hops: file->row, object->registry row, published->revision.
    Stronger prior art exists and covers one graph each: PROV-O (derivation),
      OpenLineage (runtime events), DDI-CDI (datum roles across structures).
    RETAINED: traversability, plus evidence-bound traversal - every hop must state how it
      is known (FK, digest, declaration, recorded event).

  CONTRACT-GOVERNED LIFECYCLE .............................. MODIFY
    Contracts can govern the chain without becoming a second semantic authority under one
    rule the evidence supports: a contract is authority for DECLARATION; never for
    observation (what is), acts (what was approved) or outcomes (what was measured).
    TEST: does any contract field change without a new revision? Then it is state
    masquerading as declaration - which is exactly CAP-011.
    THREE STAGES MISSING from the proposed 12: APPROVAL/AUTHORIZATION,
    CORRECTION/REPROCESSING (a governed loop back from published), RETIREMENT.
